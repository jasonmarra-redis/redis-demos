Stage 1: The Architecture
Experian Health's Real-Time Claims Decisioning Stack
Five tiers form a continuous loop: ingest, context, decide, act, learn. Experian Health's source systems feed Redis. Redis decides. The registrar sees a verified, eligibility-confirmed, denial-scored claim recommendation in under 10 milliseconds, before the patient finishes checking in.
Powered by Redis Context Surfaces: Assembling patient identity, 1,700+ payer feeds, historical claim patterns, and AI Advantage denial models into a single decision package in under 10ms.
