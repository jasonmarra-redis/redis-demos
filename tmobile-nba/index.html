Stage 1: The Architecture
T-Mobile's Next Best Action Stack on Redis
Five tiers form a continuous loop: ingest, context, decide, act, learn. Cassandra, Postgres, and Databricks feed Redis through RDI and Featureform. Redis decides in under 10 milliseconds. T-Life, care, web, mobile, and SMS all render the same decision. This architecture replaces Pega ADP entirely and closes the 300-to-900 millisecond latency gap that cannot be tuned away.

Stage 2: Call Trigger
Alina calls into a Team of Experts
A high-value Magenta MAX customer with a multi-line account opens a care session. The moment the call lands, the expert's desktop fires a decision request. Redis has roughly ten milliseconds to return a decision before the screen renders. Today on Pega ADP, the decision returns in 420 milliseconds and the expert gets a stale, generic script.

Why This Call Matters
Alina is in the top decile of household ARPU. An 11-year tenure with 4 lines is the kind of relationship that compounds for another decade, or unwinds in a single bad call.
Her network signal data already flags a coverage problem at home. She is calling right now because of it. The expert needs to see that on the first screen, not surface it after a three-minute diagnostic.
The opportunity: Lead with a signal booster at zero cost, layer Home Internet on top (her address is UC-eligible), and convert a retention risk into an expansion event. All of this has to be decided before the expert says hello.
On Pega ADP today: 420 ms decision returns after the expert screen loads. Script reads "we value your business." No network context, no HSI propensity, no attribution loop. Alina hangs up with a credit on her account and a still-broken indoor signal.
