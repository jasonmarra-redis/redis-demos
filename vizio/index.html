Why This Matters

Stage 7: Revenue Optimization
Personalization meets monetization
WatchFree+ content receives a revenue multiplier because every view generates ad impressions. The system balances what Marcus wants to watch with what generates revenue for Vizio.
ML RankingLangCache
Revenue-Weighted Ranking Logic
WatchFree+ revenue per view$0.038 avg CPM per minute
Netflix/Prime revenue per view$0.00 (no ad revenue)
WF+ priority multiplier applied1.4x for high-relevance WF+ content
Personalization quality floorNever below 70% relevance
The key insight: A WatchFree+ title at 88% relevance generates more total value than a Netflix title at 92% relevance, because the WF+ view creates ad revenue while Netflix creates zero. Redis optimizes for total viewer value, not just click-through rate.
