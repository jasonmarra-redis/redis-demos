Stage 1: The Architecture
Banco Tavola's Real-Time Pix Decisioning Stack
Five tiers form a continuous loop: ingest, context, decide, act, learn. Tavola's source systems feed Redis. Redis decides. The Pix authorization channel acts. Every Pix transaction passes through this architecture in under 80 milliseconds, well inside the BCB 100ms SLA.
REDIS CONTEXT SURFACES
The framing layer for the demo. Redis assembles the right context from five disparate systems and hands it to the decision engine in under 10 milliseconds. Every capability you see in this demo is a surface on top of that single primitive.
