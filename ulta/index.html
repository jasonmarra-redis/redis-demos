Stage 1: The Architecture
Ulta's Real-Time Decisioning Stack
Five tiers form a continuous loop: ingest, context, decide, act, learn. Ulta's Adobe CDP, commerce, and behavioral systems feed Redis. Redis decides. Ulta.com and the mobile app render. Every personalization decision for 46M+ loyalty members flows through this architecture in under 10 milliseconds, with in-session behavioral signals reflected immediately, not on the next page load.

Why This Matters
Sarah is one of 46M+ loyalty members who drive 95% of Ulta sales. Diamond members visit 3x more often and spend 5x more than the average guest.
Her last session ended without a purchase. The in-session signal (mascara browsing, abandoned at PDP) is the most predictive data Ulta has, and it's only useful if it's reflected right now, not on tomorrow's email.
The opportunity: Recognize the in-session intent, cross it with her Beauty Graph affinity, and surface a complete eye routine in the moment, not after the session ends.
Without Redis: Sarah sees the same generic home screen as every other guest. Static "Trending in Beauty" carousel. Mascara browsing signal sits in a batch queue. She closes the app and the moment is gone.

Stage 7: LTV Optimization
Personalization meets lifetime value
Ulta optimizes for guest lifetime value, not just this session's basket. Diamond loyalty members get a routine-completion boost, replenishment-overdue products get a recency multiplier, and the model balances immediate conversion against long-term loyalty health.
ML RankingRules Engine
LTV-Weighted Ranking Logic
Diamond tier LTV multiplier1.3x on routine-completion plays
Replenishment overdue boost1.25x on category-overdue SKUs
Birthday window boost1.2x on premium / gift-eligible SKUs
Frequency cap (same brand)Max 2 SKUs per brand on home
Personalization quality floorNever below 75% relevance
The key insight: A 3-product eye routine (mascara + palette + primer) at $92 AOV generates 2.5x more LTV than the single $26 mascara, because routine completion drives the next visit and the visit after that. Redis optimizes for the loyalty flywheel, not just this checkout.
