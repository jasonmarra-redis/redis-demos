Why This Moment Matters
Elena is a tier-one private client. $4.2M AUM, 11-year tenure, referred two other households worth $6.8M combined. The kind of client Meridian cannot afford to lose.
HNW churn is almost never a surprise trade. It is months of behavioral drift: more logins, longer dwells on Performance, held-away balances growing, meeting cancellations. By the time the ACAT request arrives, the decision was made weeks ago.
The cost of getting this wrong: Industry HNW attrition runs 5-8% annually. One lost $4.2M household is roughly $42K in lost revenue per year, compounded across a 20-year relationship. Meridian loses $840K in projected fees plus the referral network behind her.
Without Redis: Addepar holds the portfolio. Salesforce holds the meeting notes. Aladdin holds the risk model. Plaid holds the held-away balances. By the time the advisor stitches them together, the meeting is over and the moment is gone.
Stage 3: Ingest
