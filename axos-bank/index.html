Why This Matters
Elena holds $184K in deposits across four Axos products. She is exactly the customer Axos acquires through M&A and monetizes through cross-sell.
Her Axos Invest account sits on a different core than her checking. Her legacy CD came from an acquired institution on a third system. Every time she opens the app, the dashboard has to fan out across all three and aggregate.
The opportunity: Elena is on the phone with a financial planner in 12 minutes. If the unified view loads in 180ms instead of 8 seconds, she stays engaged, sees the cross-sell, and deepens the relationship.
Without Redis: Elena sees a spinner for 8 seconds while Axos ONE makes synchronous calls to 3 different core systems. She abandons. The planning call happens with her net worth quoted from Fidelity instead.
Stage 3: Ingest
Axos's disparate cores flow into Redis
RDI uses change data capture to continuously sync every acquired core into Redis. Featureform orchestrates ML features from Databricks and Kafka. One unified ingest layer. Zero nightly batch. Zero custom .NET sync code.
