Stage 2: App Session Start
Jasmine starts a breakfast order on the McDonald's app
A session event fires while Jasmine is seven minutes from her usual drive-thru. Redis has one decision window to pick the right breakfast path, frame the right value construct, and line up prep against her arrival.

Why This Matters
McDonald's is leaning harder into value and loyalty at the same time. The job is not simply to show breakfast items. It is to choose the breakfast path that fits Jasmine's routine, budget, and arrival moment.
Jasmine is close enough that the decision now affects restaurant operations, not just the app. If prep timing is wrong, the order sits. If prep timing is right, the handoff feels effortless.
The moment: pick the right breakfast value path and decide whether Ready on Arrival should fire before Jasmine enters the lane.
Without Redis: the app shows static breakfast tiles, the crew does not see intent until checkout, and restaurant prep starts too late or too early.

Stage 7: Business Optimization
One decision balances conversion, value, and throughput
The winner is not just the meal Jasmine is likely to buy. It is the meal and prep timing that fit the restaurant's current state, the value narrative McDonald's is pushing, and the loyalty outcome McDonald's wants to reinforce.

Prep triggerReady on Arrival at T-3 min

Suppression rulesLunch offers, unavailable items, slow-prep bundles

The key insight: a value decision only works if restaurant execution agrees with it. Redis turns guest relevance and restaurant readiness into one decision, then uses Streams to keep the app, the crew tablet, and the kitchen aligned as Jasmine approaches the store.
